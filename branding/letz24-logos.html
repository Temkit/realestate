My recommendation

Route 01 as the logo, Route 02 as the app icon.

The Tréma Pin is the most ownable: it grows straight out of the name's own letterform, says "local Luxembourg" and "find a service near me" in one shape, and stays legible as pure line-art at any size and in one color. Pair it with the 24 Roundel for the app icon and favicon, where a bold, filled shape reads better on a home screen. Both carry the single red dot — that's the thread that ties the whole identity together, even when the wordmark isn't present. Flag Shield is the strong runner-up if you want to lean harder into national identity.
